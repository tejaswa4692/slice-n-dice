VEFECTS - POO VFX for GODOT
CHANGELOG

1.0.3 (2026-10-21)
- New Shared/Scripts/Vefects_VFX_Controller.gd: attached to any parent node, it plays, pauses, stops and restarts every
  particle system under it from Inspector buttons, in the editor and in game, and can replay them every few
  seconds (Auto Replay), one-shot effects included.

1.0.2 (2026-10-19)
- Particle shader: the values driven by custom data curves (erosion, dissolve...) now decode exactly at the
  start and end of the curve. Before, a particle whose erosion curve reached exactly 0 or 1 could be drawn as
  a full square instead of disappearing.

1.0.1 (2026-10-17)
- LICENSE.txt: added the copyright year and stated explicitly that the effects may not be sold or
  redistributed as transformative or derivative works. No changes to the effects.

1.0.0 (2026-10-17)
- First release for Godot 4.7.
