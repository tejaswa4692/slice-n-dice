Holy fuck this should be 3rd devlog of this game but today i got one of the most undocumented and most annoying things to work

I got blenders rigify root motion working properly in godot

heres how i did it

First make the animation you want to create
Normally blender doesnt have all DEF bones as child of root thats why some bones keep moving forward
to fix that go to search in your outliner in blender and in that look for MCH-torso.parent bone
Go in edit mode and select that bone, then reparent it to root, Now you will see that all your DEF bones work with root motion
but theres an issue, when you move the root forward the bones we just added have double transform
to fix that select the MCH-torso.parent in pose mode then go in bone constraitns it will already have a SWITCH_PARENT constraint added to it, set the influence from 1 to a 0 and your double transform will be fixed

Now when importing to godot firsly select your root bone and then in bone properties add it as a deform bone
and then when exporting it as gltf remember to export only deform bones, and boom it will work now
